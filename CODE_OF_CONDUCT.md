# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in NEXORA to be welcoming, respectful, constructive, and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful.
- Discuss disagreements constructively.
- Give actionable feedback focused on the work.
- Respect different experience levels and communication styles.
- Help keep discussions welcoming.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope and reporting

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related communication. Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's available maintainer contact channel. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that do not meet these standards.
